Requirements

What you need before you attach it.

01
MT4 or MT5 terminal + broker account

Any broker that supports expert advisors works. UMNi-BOT trades XAUUSD — check your broker offers it before signing up.

02
Algo trading enabled

In your terminal: Tools → Options → Expert Advisors, tick "Allow Algo Trading," then confirm the AutoTrading toggle in the toolbar is green.

03
WebRequest URL whitelisted

Add api.telegram.org to your allowed URLs list (same Expert Advisors options tab) — this is how the EA talks to your personal bot.

04
Your own Telegram bot token

Message @BotFather on Telegram, send /newbot, and follow the prompts. Takes under a minute and it's free — this becomes your bot's private line to your account.

05
An active UMNi subscription

Join via Whop below. Once you're in, send your bot token over and your instance gets activated within 24 hours.
