FAQ
Is this real exchange volume?

On most forex, metals and CFD feeds brokers don't publish who bought or sold, so FLOW estimates pressure from the direction of every price change (the tick rule). On MT5 exchange symbols that publish trade side and volume, it uses the real traded volume.

Will it spam me?

No. Pressure must hold for 2 closed bars before it alerts, and it only re-arms once it fades. Spikes and surges are one alert per minute or bar, with a cooldown.

Is it a trade signal?

No. It's a read on activity and momentum to support your own decisions.

How do I install it?

Buy it on the MQL5 Market, install it from the Market tab inside MetaTrader, allow WebRequest for https://api.telegram.org under Tools > Options > Expert Advisors, attach it to a chart and enter your Telegram bot token and chat ID.
